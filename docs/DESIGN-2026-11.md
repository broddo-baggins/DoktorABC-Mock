# Design direction, November 2026 polish pass

Mode: redesign, preserve. The "Dispensary" system in `DESIGN.md` (pine on cool paper, Schibsted Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono, pharmacy-label motif) already carries the identity. This pass keeps it and fixes what still weakens it.

Design read: four-portal health platform concept for recruiters and engineers scanning a portfolio, with a calm clinical language, on the existing token system. Dials: variance 5, motion 4, density 4 public and 6 in the portals.

## Direction

- One accent (pine), used for the primary action, links and "done" only.
- Type scale stays as in `DESIGN.md` (12 to 60 px); mono for every figure someone acts on.
- Calm density: flat cards with a hairline, shadows only on floating things.
- Motion is feedback or ordering only, and every animation is gated by `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- AA contrast, a visible 2 px focus ring, 44 px targets, labelled fields (already enforced by the axe e2e).
- No em dashes in UI copy. Sample data only; the badge says so on every page.
