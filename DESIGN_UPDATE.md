## 🎨 **New Color Palette**

### **Primary Colors** (Pink/Rose Theme)
```

- primary-200: #fbcfe8  (Light rose)
- primary-600: #db2777  (Deep pink - primary actions)
- primary-700: #be185d  (Darker pink - hover states)
```

### **Accent Colors** (Teal/Cyan for CTAs)
```
- accent-50:  #f0fdfa  (Light teal)
- accent-500: #14b8a6  (Teal - primary CTAs)

```

### **Neutral Colors** (Modern grays)
```
- neutral-50:  #fafafa  (Nearly white)
- neutral-900: #171717  (Almost black for text)
```

**Replaces:** Previous blue (#3b82f6) and green (#22c55e) scheme
